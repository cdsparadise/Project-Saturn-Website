Story

Sonic

Project Saturn Story

A superpowered meteor has fallen from the sky onto Sonic’s planet. Dr. Robotnik has managed to get a hold of it and soon discovers that it is more than a meteor, but a parasitic lifeform in itself with incredible and dangerous power, dubbed the “Ridicule Root.”

He embeds its powerful core into that of Metal Sonic to charge up his master plan to drain the world of its energy and build his empire atop its ashes. To further power the root, the doctor uses its power to seek out the Chaos Emeralds.

“If Robotnik thinks he can control the Root, then he’s about to learn what real power looks like!”

The fate of the planet now rests on the heroes’ ability to stop this twisted fusion of technology and ancient cosmic force before it consumes everything in its path.

What begins as a battle for the world soon becomes a struggle against the corruption of power itself...
